2. QuickBooks Online Data

We provide bookkeeping, financial reporting, and analytics services to clients who use QuickBooks Online. With a client's authorization, we access that client's QuickBooks Online company file through Intuit's API. This section describes how we handle that data.

How Access Works

A client authorizes us through Intuit's OAuth 2.0 process. The client signs in to their own QuickBooks Online account and approves the connection. We never receive or store the client's QuickBooks username or password. The client can revoke our access at any time from their QuickBooks Online account settings, or by contacting us.

Information We Access

Depending on the permissions granted and the reports needed for the engagement, we may access:

  • Company information (name, address, fiscal year settings, and company identifiers)
  • Chart of accounts
  • Customers, vendors, employees, and items
  • Invoices, bills, payments, bill payments, and journal entries
  • Financial reports, such as Profit and Loss, General Ledger, and Cash Flow

How We Use QuickBooks Online Data

We use QuickBooks Online data only to provide the services the client has engaged us for, including preparing reports, reconciliations, summaries, and analyses for that client. This includes building internal dashboards and reports in Microsoft Power BI from the exported data. We do not use QuickBooks Online data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

How We Store and Protect QuickBooks Online Data

  • OAuth refresh tokens are stored in a restricted file on our secured systems, separate from exported client data. They are not included in source code or shared.
  • Application credentials, including the Client ID and Client Secret, are stored as environment variables, not in code.
  • Exported financial data (CSV files) is stored in client-specific folders on our local computer, accessible only to us.
